The house

built 1897, glass and iron

A glassmaker's fever dream that outlived him. Verdant House was raised in 1897 to hold the palm seeds brought back from one voyage around the Cape, and it has held plants in glass ever since.

The building was restored in 2019, pane by pane, by the city and four hundred neighbours who each paid for a pane. The glass is new. Everything growing under it is older than anyone who visits.
